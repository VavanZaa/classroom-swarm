Match the latter profiles to the declared grading context and also report automation versus combined costly grading. These coarse costs can overwhelm a small teacher budget; backlog and feedback timing are outcomes, not hidden queue truncation. The completed design has **161 classes per replicate, 3,864 new classroom runs**, plus thirteen historical replay checks.

Each size/assignment arm uses the same total resources under a given profile. A31 currently has one teacher identity per section; coordinated teacher types and cross-section decisions remain missing. Convexity is a hypothesis inspired by the paper's theory, not an identified field contract.

Student panels: mechanism-off baseline, topics/consolidation, peer noise/delay, combined student mechanisms. Teacher incentives/workload are not added to teacher-free lab contexts. E14 AI/seating first receive a separate joint-practice bridge with all A31 features off because active A31 features do not support legacy hints. ## Analysis and continuity checks

Replay thirteen declared historical classrooms using their original seeds/configurations with mechanisms off and compare every original raw table (empty placeholder rows normalized). These replays establish numerical continuity; fresh mechanism-off replicates supply the contemporaneous baseline. Historical reports remain separate evidence with their original sample sizes/intervals.

For each predeclared study contrast and profile, report exam/homework/knowledge and activity/time differences. Also report the paired difference in that treatment contrast relative to its matched mechanism-off context. This difference-of-differences answers whether A31 changes the previous model conclusion; it is not a human effect estimate. Resolve all global cohorts/subgroups before treatment; aggregate school sections weighted by student count.

Use 5,000 paired bootstrap resamples, seed 10072199; report pointwise intervals throughout and Bonferroni simultaneous intervals across the eleven main exam contrasts for the combined-versus-reference change. Zero means and signs near numerical roundoff are treated as zero within 1e-10. Composition and teacher-reward probes are exploratory and outside that primary family. A 1-model-pp equivalence band is declared only as a simulator diagnostic; a human nonsignificant result does not establish equivalence. Negative, changed, unchanged and uncertain effects all remain in the report. No re-selection, extension of replicate count, or coefficient edits based on outcomes.

Encoded identities: reward payments grant no knowledge; direct topic gains/transfer/consolidation are nonnegative; fatigue lowers declared effective skill; teacher time is bounded; no received peer signal means no quality update; turning all mechanisms off preserves A30; scheduled session frequency is fixed; seating has no financial student incentive; all existing copying/AI provenance and learning assumptions remain unchanged.
