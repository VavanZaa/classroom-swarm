# E20 — A31 functional mechanism diagnostics

The user explicitly deferred human calibration and authorized teacher incentives, workload, topics and peer observation. This plan checks their implementation and interactions without tuning to study signs. A31 is specified in [docs/21](../21-v4-classroom-mechanisms.md); every prior source/run is preserved.

Freeze source/settings hashes and both seed pools before outcomes. Run all 24 conditions on 6 development replicates (seed 10062011) and all 24 on 18 fresh replicates (10062012): **576 classrooms**. No selection, coefficient adjustment or stopping on a desired sign is permitted within this identity. If a defect appears, preserve the partial root and source snapshot, declare the correction and use a new root/model identity as applicable.

Eight students, six 16-tick episodes, shared practice, static degree-four ER contacts, one eight-tick teacher budget, observed adaptive instruction/guidance, 12 homework items, 30 measurement-only exam items each episode. Copying and AI are disabled to expose the new channels. Alpha 0.0584 and effort 0.15 are assumed trajectories/preferences, not human estimates. Every condition shares its replicate graph and semantic draws; module-induced scheduling changes can change realized item slots. Two-topic conditions split exam items equally and clone initial scalar knowledge across both topics; no hidden initial-score advantage is introduced.

Profiles are fixed in `scripts/e20_mechanism_diagnostic.py`: A30 reference; linear/zero/concave/convex rewards and cheaper effort; admin bursts, costly grading and automated grading; fatigue and zero-fatigue control; isolated topics, explicit cross-transfer, topic preparation and helper consolidation; perfect, noisy, delayed and absent peer signals; combined features and four one-module removals. Each child has an explicit parent contrast. Setting bundles acknowledge that their parts cannot be identified separately. Disabling topics removes transfer/consolidation and changes the knowledge representation; that removal is a whole-module contrast.

Record final all-item exam and mean topic knowledge, teacher ticks, returned grades, peer updates/backlog, fatigue and paid rewards. Re-read every saved raw hash and run the complete topic/observed-policy/time/grade/provenance validator. Compare each child to its parent with paired bootstrap intervals (3,000 resamples, seed 10062099). These are descriptive functional/sensitivity results with many correlated comparisons; intervals are Monte Carlo uncertainty and do not establish human efficacy. Keep every positive, negative and uncertain difference. Confirmation repeats the full design without selection.

Encoded identities are listed in the declaration: positive direct learning/transfer/consolidation, private signal arrival limits, budget constraints, deterministic payments and fatigue skill penalties. Splitting fixed exposure among independent skills changes the interpretation of aggregate competence. None should be claimed as an empirical discovery. Net exam differences, teacher policy choices, feedback completion and activity substitution must be interpreted with their recorded schedules and assumed rates.
