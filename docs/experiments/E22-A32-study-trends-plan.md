# E22 — A32 compared with published study trends

This plan and the complete runner, report generator, tests, all engine sources, and every resolved classroom declaration are frozen before the first outcome. New seed 100772201, 24 fresh paired school/cohort replicates, bootstrap seed 100772299, 20,000 paired resamples. Default profiles are primary. Sensitivities are declared here and every result remains visible. No outcome-driven selection, coefficient adjustment or human calibration. A32 and older locked sources/reports remain unchanged.

The question is whether the simulation generates paper-consistent directions under explicit study-informed assumptions, and whether observed activity supports a plausible explanation. These are known-direction probes: the papers already informed the model and design. Agreement is not independent validation of a causal model or evidence that the same policy works in humans.

## Protocols and assumptions

Common settings: alpha 0.0584 and effort aversion 0.15 are constant simulator hypotheses; confidence, control and benefit priors 0.5. Twelve worksheet items and sixty unaided exam items; exams every H/4 blocks (integer), feedback delay zero. No copying except the explicit composition context. Peer contacts are frozen. Learning/attention/processing/fatigue use A32 defaults, with no guaranteed competence credit. Exam measurement is frozen across arms. Full numerical defaults are resolved and stored in each job file; this prose is not a substitute for those declarations.

## Estimands, pairing and inference

Student global identities and trait/response draws stay stable across reassignments. One complete fixed school/cohort is the resampling unit; classrooms and students are not treated as independent replicates. All sections within a paper case share the same semantic seed and replicate; classroom-level teacher random streams are also shared, a variance-reduction assumption, not independent human teacher heterogeneity. Exact initial knowledge and observed-baseline subgroups are frozen before treatment; slot remapping follows global IDs. Subgroups never use realized activity or later scores.

Primary: small-minus-regular core exam; tracked-minus-mixed for both starting halves; answer-minus-none (negative), tutor-minus-answer (positive), tutor-minus-none (near-zero diagnostic); single voluntary mixed-minus-tracked and mixed-minus-independent for lower students; seating-only low, rewarded-minus-unrewarded cross-seating low and high; teacher-reward familiar potential score, unrelated potential score, familiar participation. Fourteen contrasts. The repeated lab and peer-mean cases and reward controls are secondary. Changing peer groups/seating is an assignment intervention that necessarily changes contacts; these contrasts identify the declared bundle, not isolated distribution moments.

All effects carry pointwise paired percentile-bootstrap 95% Monte Carlo intervals. The fourteen default primary contrasts additionally carry Bonferroni-adjusted intervals (alpha .05/14). A nonzero mean whose interval crosses zero is uncertain, not replicated. A declared ±1 simulator percentage-point band only diagnoses practical near-zero behavior within this model; a human nonsignificant result is not proof of equivalence and simulator points do not convert to SDs, percentiles, puzzle time or human grades. Intervals describe random variation under fixed assumptions, not uncertainty about model realism.

Potential unaided performance (everyone's fixed item opportunity), observed participants' scores, enrolled-cohort contribution (missing=0), participation, competence gains, productive doses, teacher time, guidance/coaching/followup, paid study cost and helper sessions are separate. No zero is substituted for unavailable forms/participant means. When participation changes, a cohort-score gain is not automatically knowledge learning. Hypothetical potential outcomes are analyst diagnostics, unavailable to teacher/student policies.

Known identities: copied answers, exams and payments have no direct knowledge effect; zero topic/form transfer blocks direct cross-topic/form effects; protected instruction is a fixed opportunity; repeated shared-practice opportunity frequency is scheduled; missing=0 depresses cohort means. Report these as encoded restrictions. The size/sign of total contrasts involving displaced activity and uptake remains an outcome.

## Execution and preservation

Meaningful experiment-integrity tests check identity remapping, observed subgroup/reward assignments, single-setting controls, weighted aggregation/missingness, fixed paired inference and complete contrast coverage. Strict configuration preflight precedes the lock. Each raw classroom is saved, hash-verified, reloaded and numerically validated before analysis. Resuming can reuse only hash-verified complete outputs; incomplete folders are preserved under distinct names. Design/source/declaration mismatches stop execution. Simulation multiprocessing changes execution only, not draws.

Every profile and contrary result is reported; no best-profile selection. E21, A32 functional and older source locks are verified. Saved E21 effects provide historical descriptive reference only: E22 changes learning, resources and paper protocols jointly, so cross-version differences cannot isolate an A32 mechanism effect. No old-source rerun is required because E21 already performed thirteen exact historical replays.

If a failure reveals an engine defect, preserve source/raw bytes and register a new correction identity before rerunning. Ordinary model mismatch is reported with the missing mechanism or unsupported assumption. Do not change frozen physics merely to get a paper's sign.
