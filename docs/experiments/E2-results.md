## Predictions
- **Pa (β_P ≤ 1.5 changes pC_late by less than δ): confirmed.** −0.0008 and +0.0017, both below δ.
- **Pb (p_C⁰ = 0.20: pC_late about +0.09, no growth above δ): direction confirmed, magnitude underestimated.** pC_late rose +0.113, not about +0.09. The growth contrast is imprecise under the rule and not interpreted, although its whole CI [+0.0005, +0.0107] lies below +δ.

## Other outcomes reaching δ (all adequately precise)
- **p_C⁰** is the dominant lever. At 0.20: more reach (+0.110), lower knowledge (K_final −0.022), and less concentrated routes (CF −0.20). At 0.01, the reverse: K_final +0.015, cascades almost vanish (Lmax −0.72).
- **β_P = 6:** routes are less concentrated (CF −0.061).
- **q₀ = 0:** routes are more concentrated (CF +0.063).
- **Plausible max:** knowledge falls (K_final −0.031), reach rises (+0.151), cascades deepen (Lmax +0.28; largest cascade 2.87 against 2.11), routes are less concentrated (CF −0.22).
- **Grade–knowledge decoupling stays below δ in every condition**, including plausible max (dHK_late 0.0079, dGK_last 0.0079).

## Answers to the preregistered questions
Under these mechanisms and conditions:
1. **Peer influence:** copying rises only weakly with β_P. **Even at β_P = 6** (12× the reference, 4× the plausible maximum), copying *level* rises by only 1.8 points, and copying *growth* stays below δ (+0.003). Peer contagion does not take off.
2. **Reinforcement levers:** κ and η_Q move copying by less than 1 point, and never produce growth above δ.
3. **Seed prevalence p_C⁰ sets the copying level almost one-for-one.** Copying in V1 is mostly set by the initial bias $b_C$, not generated by the dynamics.
4. **q₀ matters:** q₀ = 0 lowers copying by 1.5 points (and its growth by 1.2), with q₀ = 0.5 the opposite. That's below δ, but any claim about persistent regimes must carry the q₀ caveat.
5. **Plausible max:** the only condition where copying *grows* over time (0.227 → 0.269; growth +0.049 against the reference, CI [+0.043, +0.055], entirely above δ). **By the locked rule this growth can't be claimed**: the CI half-width 0.0059 exceeds the δ/4 limit of 0.0050 by 18%. A preregistered E2b with about 300 replicates would settle it, since the half-width shrinks as $1/\sqrt n$ (0.0059 × √(200/300) ≈ 0.0048).

## Interpretation (post hoc, for Vavan's decision)
- **Why "my friend copies, so I copy" doesn't happen in V1:** the peer signal $X$ measures *neighbours who asked me for answers*, not *friends who copied*. A friend who copies from someone else is invisible to me, and being asked is rare (mean $X$ = 0.018). So even a huge β_P has almost nothing to amplify. The model encodes the blueprint's §7 definition faithfully; that definition just isn't the mechanism in the question. Testing it needs a **model revision** (e.g. $X_i$ = share of neighbours who chose C in the previous homework), which is a V2 spec change, not a parameter.
- **Rule note:** for plausible max, the precision rule withheld a claim whose whole CI lies more than 2δ above zero. The rule behaves exactly as locked and validated, but it may be stricter than needed when an effect is far from the decision boundary. That's a design question for future preregistrations, not a change to E2.
