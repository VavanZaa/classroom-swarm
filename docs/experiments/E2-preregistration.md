# E2 · Preregistration: what would it take for copying to grow? (LOCKED)

**Locked 2026-09-18 before any E2 code or run existed.** Designed and locked by Claude under Vavan's explicit delegation of the same day ("do decision 1 and 2 and then run another experiment based on that"). Any later change is a numbered deviation (§10).

## 0. Background (post hoc, from E1: this is why E2 exists)
E1 ([[docs/experiments/E1-results]]) was null at the reference parameters: copying stayed at about the seeded 10%. A post hoc diagnosis (`scripts/diagnose_e1_copying.py`, commit `1f15866`, 50 replicates, SBM, R=3) found:
- Copying pays: a successful copy earns reward 0.706, against 0.556 (S) and 0.560 (D).
- But copying is rarely tried (about 2 of 20 homeworks per student; 14% never), so $Q^C$ is updated rarely and ends at 0.468, below $Q^S$ 0.561 and $Q^D$ 0.565.
- The peer signal is tiny: mean $X=0.018$ (90% of student-homeworks have $X=0$), so $\beta_P X\approx0.009$ utility.
- Moving $P(C)$ from 0.10 to 0.20 needs about +0.81 utility; to 0.30, +1.35. Copying's reward edge is capped near $\kappa=0.15$.

These are hypotheses generated by looking at E1. E2 tests them on **new data** (new seed) with a design fixed here.

## 1. Questions
1. **Peer influence (Vavan's question):** how do copying prevalence and growth change with $\beta_P\in\{0,0.5,1.5,3,6\}$? 3 and 6 are **stress levels** beyond the §32 plausible range (0–1.5), included to find how strong peer influence must be before copying spreads.
2. **Reinforcement levers:** what do effort-cost aversion $\kappa\in\{0,0.40\}$ and learning rate $\eta_Q\in\{0.05,0.40\}$ change?
3. **Seed prevalence:** $p_C^0\in\{0.01,0.20\}$.
4. **Initialisation (mandatory robustness axis, A6):** $q_0\in\{0,0.5\}$ against the derived 0.35.
5. **Plausible maximum:** with every plausible lever at its pro-copying extreme at once ($\beta_P=1.5$, $\kappa=0.40$, $\eta_Q=0.40$, $p_C^0=0.20$), does copying grow?

## 2. Predictions (from the E1 diagnosis; every outcome is reported either way)
- **Pa:** for $\beta_P\le1.5$, $\lvert\Delta\,$pC_late$\rvert<\delta$ against the reference (negligible or below δ), because $\beta_P X$ stays below about 0.03 utility.
- **Pb:** $p_C^0=0.20$ raises pC_late by about +0.09 (a mechanical seed effect), without copying *growth* (pC_change not above +δ).
- No prediction is made for $\kappa$, $\eta_Q$, $q_0$, the stress levels of $\beta_P$ or the plausible-max condition. They are open questions.

## 3. Design
- **Stage M2** (reinforcement + peer exposure), **R = 3, SBM**, `Params()` defaults except the single change in the table. The reference is E1's M2/R3/SBM condition, re-run here with E2's seed.
- **14 conditions × 200 replicates = 2,800 runs.** Per replicate: one SBM graph and one shared `Draws(R_max=3)` for every condition (common random numbers; conditions differ only in the parameter).
- **Condition label = `experiment_id` in `run_id`** (e.g. `E2-kappa0.40/rep0001/SBM/R3/M2`). Raw output goes to `runs/E2/`.
- $q_0$ follows $K_0-\kappa$ (A12), so the $\kappa$ conditions also move $q_0$ (κ 0 → 0.50, κ 0.40 → 0.10). The $q_0$ conditions vary $q_0$ at the reference κ.
- **Seeds:** master 918002, bootstrap 918200 ($B=10{,}000$), smoke 918998.

## 4. Outcomes
The **same definitions, windows and δ as E1** (E1 preregistration §4, §4b, §6), computed by the same code (`analysis.run_summaries`). Confirmatory: pC_late, pC_change, succ_late, K_final, dHK_late, dGK_last, Smax_mean, Lmax_mean, CF. Secondary: VarK_final, dHK_mean, dGK_block, reach. **Primary for Questions 1–5:** pC_late (copying level) and pC_change (copying growth). No broker null in E2 (E1 found no brokers; brokerage is not an E2 question).

## 5. Contrasts and estimation
Each of the 13 non-reference conditions minus E2-ref, paired by replicate, for every outcome: mean difference with a 95% percentile bootstrap CI on one shared resample matrix. NA pairs are dropped and $n$ is reported, exactly as in E1 §5.

## 6. Interpretation
- Labels as in E1 §6: detected (CI excludes 0) with magnitude below / straddling / above δ; negligible (CI within ±δ); inconclusive.
- **Adequacy (Decision 2): a contrast is adequately precise iff its CI half-width ≤ δ/4** (`analysis.precision_check`). This rule was validated on synthetic data before this lock (`scripts/validate_precision_rule.py`, commit `b199681`): it passes when precise, fails when not, and responds to n. That's the property the void E1 rule lacked. An imprecise confirmatory contrast is reported as "imprecise, not interpreted", and only a new preregistration may add replicates.
- **"Copying grows"** is claimed for a condition only if its pC_change contrast against the reference is detected with magnitude *above* δ (> +0.02) and is adequately precise.

## 7. Known limits
- One-factor-at-a-time: E2 can't reveal interactions between levers, except in the single plausible-max condition.
- $X$ counts neighbours who asked *me*, not friends who copied (§7 of the blueprint). E2 varies how strongly $X$ acts, not what it measures. Changing what peer exposure measures would be a model revision (V2), not an E2 condition.
- The softmax temperature is fixed at 1 (§33). E2 doesn't vary the utility scale.
- All claims: "under these mechanisms and conditions" (§49).
