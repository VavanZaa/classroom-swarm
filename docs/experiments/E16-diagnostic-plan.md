# E16: tutoring extension diagnostics, declared before outcomes

Date 2026-10-01. A29 implements the user's requested activity/content extension;
E14/E15 engines, configurations and outputs remain historical. Inspect all declared
development arms before deciding whether a code defect or missing causal mechanism
requires a separately documented model revision. Desired signs alone do not justify
changing a coefficient. Validation seeds/configurations are frozen at the same time
as development configurations and are inspected only after the development review.

Thirty development and 100 validation replicates. Seeds 10011601/10011602 for lab/
composition development and 10011691/10011692 for validation. All settings, 24
conditions and contrasts per stage are resolved and locked with source hashes.
Paired semantic streams, static graphs, all raw tables and raw audits are retained.
No selection of successful replicates or omission of contrary arms is allowed.

Lab: eight students, four fixed K=.35 starters and four K=.75 starters; mixed or
tracked groups of four, exactly the E14 graph assignments. H=20, exams every five,
no copying, confidence and benefits fixed initially at .5 to isolate composition.
Scheduled shared practice is a distinct task protocol: compare mixed/track, each
versus equal-duration independent practice, and no-helper-learning, zero teaching
skill, five-tick instead of three-tick practice, very difficult content d=.95,
legacy optional hints, and voluntary joint practice. Each probe changes one declared
setting except the difficult-content distribution, explicitly bundled because its
family and support change together. No teacher or helper reward is present. Item-specific knowledge is a
minimal numerical representation; 20 homework episodes are not the human study's
eight sessions, and the exam is not a Sudoku solving-time outcome.

The [Kimbrough, McGee and Shigeoka primary manuscript](https://www.crepe.e.u-tokyo.ac.jp/events/20190325_shigeoka.pdf)
has groups working on the same puzzle, with optional conversation, then individual
evaluation. Shared practice represents the reserved collaborative task opportunity;
it does not claim to reproduce conversational choice, visible ranks or observed
teaching frequency. Those are limitations, not coefficients adjusted after results.

Composition: N=30, ER mean degree six, focal first 15 at K=.35; other 15 at .35 or
.75. Compare joint practice versus legacy hints with copying off/on. This is a
generic diagnostic, not a universal literature prediction. Copying can retain a
negative peer effect even if content quality improves. All focal contrasts use
predefined student indices, not behavior-dependent subgroups.

Primary directional targets: low starters' mixed shared practice versus tracked
shared practice; mixed shared practice versus mixed independent practice. Examine
both final unaided exam and latent knowledge gain. Similar directions require both
means positive in held-out seeds, with their uncertainty reported. Do not interpret
matching signs as empirical validation. Report every ablation and protocol contrast,
including longer duration and hard content, which may reverse the total effect.
The scheduled teaching-versus-solo comparison partly encodes a nonnegative bonus
at equal base practice; state this identity, rather than treating it as a discovery.

Analysis: same final-homework time for homework and exam; paired replicate bootstrap
95% Monte Carlo intervals, 3000 resamples, exploratory pointwise intervals without
multiplicity correction. Decompose realized gain into practice, content and helper
terms as an accounting identity, not independent causal mediation. Log observed
quality, usable content, sessions, attempts and copying alongside outcome effects.

The STAR class-size effect, Duflo et al. instructional adaptation and Wu et al.
helper-reward intervention need subsequent, separately specified models. A29 cannot
claim to reproduce those interventions. Changing N without teacher capacity is
still a network-only class-size diagnostic. Existing AI pathways are unchanged.
