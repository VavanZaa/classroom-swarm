The A31 compatibility comparator retains legacy initialization streams and therefore has a different realized cohort. Its final knowledge is descriptive, not a paired causal effect against A32. Its paid-cost entry is unavailable because it lacks the new cost ledger; it does not mean zero study cost. All declared A32 single-parameter contrasts share stable initial draws. The student `format_only` condition still permits exploratory solo/revision activities; its small conceptual gain comes from those recorded activities. Pure-format teacher coaching supplies the exact concept/core null control below.

Zero-effort and zero-attention classrooms paid activity costs but had no conceptual gains. Removing useful self materials and self-generated reasoning eliminated direct self-practice gains; teacher opportunities remained separate. Every teacher work schedule conserved the declared total and retained ordinary protected instruction. Tax-only and productive preparation spent the same resource schedule.

Helper rewards, zero prizes, placebo payments, refusal, high cost, fractional quotas and failed engagement all have separate records. Helping reserves both students; payments use released observed partner progress and do not directly change knowledge. Differences in conceptual outcomes include displaced own study, explanation quality and recipient uptake, so a positive net effect is not assumed.

Pure-format coaching left conceptual states and core potential item outcomes exactly equal to its zero-reward control. This is an encoded separation, not empirical evidence for a field mechanism. Participation changes observed/cohort scores without changing the underlying potential test; missing observations remain missing. Participant-only means, full-cohort contributions and participation are separate in the saved report data.

Limits: teacher planning uses declared productivity/uptake priors and observed score proxies; it is not a calibrated model of human engagement. Mixed-topic/form scores can bias those proxies. One teacher does not represent a school tournament. Dialogue, friendship/personality effects, multiple-teacher coordination and human-unit mapping remain absent. The next scientific step is new frozen study protocols, retaining the contrary E21 cases and separating infrastructure, resources, participation and learning.
