## Reading
- Copying growth in V1 needs **every plausible lever at its pro-copying extreme at once**. No single lever produced it (E2).
- Growing copying costs knowledge (K_final −0.030, above δ), deepens cascades (depth +0.28) and spreads flow over more routes (CF −0.22).
- **Grade–knowledge decoupling stays below δ even here** (Δ_HK +0.005, Δ_GK +0.005).
- Peer exposure in V1 still measures being asked, not friends copying, so this growth comes from the combined reinforcement levers and the higher seed, not from friend contagion. Friend contagion is the V2 question (E3).
