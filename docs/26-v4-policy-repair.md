# A34: audited policy correction

Date: 2026-10-10. Authorized by the user's request to correct the audited engine, rerun multiple studies and fit parameters that make sense. A32/A33 and all historical locks remain immutable. A34 is implemented in `v4_repaired.py`, `repaired_runtime.py`, `repaired_validation.py`, and `repaired_config.py` (schema 4).

The correction reconnects existing private states. For attempt and joint hint choices, the effort evaluator receives `confidence_weight * benefit_action + (1-confidence_weight) * confidence`. This is the existing blend's meaning: weight on action-specific returned-grade beliefs. It is not a true-knowledge observation. Grade updates subsequently change these same inputs.

For hint requests, expected utility is `access_hint * (guided_practice_utility - current_packet_estimate) - request_cost`. Guided utility uses productive guided-practice duration, not request time. Every request incurs minimum operational effort at the declared guided cost multiplier, whether served or failed; the raw cost ledger records this as `hint_request`. A served request subsequently incurs its guided-practice effort. This aligns the anticipated and charged durations instead of charging productive effort on request time or leaving request costs only in a prediction. No improvement is assumed on failure. Access is the private observed service belief, not perfect knowledge of future helper availability. Teacher guidance is added to attempt preference after this calculation, as in A33. Other activity utility definitions are unchanged.

Effective initial knowledge is the mean of the final declared component matrix after overrides/expansion. Every `from_knowledge` confidence or benefit prior uses this effective value. The original declared base values remain in the manifest and curriculum projection for provenance. Explicit independent priors remain explicit. Mean-preserving default projection is unchanged.

Repairs apply even with all A33 design switches off. `repairs_enabled=False` delegates to A33 exactly, including its A32 delegation. Enabled repairs require productive learning. No new teaching action, financial reward, hidden-information channel, background learning or study-specific treatment coefficient is added.

`policy_inputs` records the private choice inputs and evaluated attempt/hint utilities. Validation checks coverage against choice events and reconstructs the blend, service-weighted score and guidance bonus. An independent audit additionally reconstructs every pretest from semantic draws, requires complete skill/pretest/readiness/lesson ledgers, reconstructs start-time prerequisites, and reconstructs teacher lesson observations from released evidence. Legacy accounting, learning, group preparation and teacher budget validators also run. Missing records now fail validation.

Broad bounds describe admissible exploratory hypotheses, not estimates from the human studies.
